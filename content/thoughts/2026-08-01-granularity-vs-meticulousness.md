---
title: Granularity vs. Meticulousness
date: 2026-08-01
summary: Structure describes the target. Execution determines whether it exists.

Granularity is judgment: what resolution should the work have, given the
result you want? Meticulousness is fidelity: once that resolution is chosen,
does the result actually realize it? A grinder makes the distinction visible.
Choosing a fine or coarse grind for the cup you want is granularity. Producing
a tight distribution instead of boulders and fines is meticulousness. The
setting can be right while the grind is wrong. The same is true of systems,
plans, and almost everything else. More detail gives work a finer shape, not
higher quality. Choose the wrong granularity and you can perfect the wrong
problem. Choose the right one without meticulousness and the intended result
exists only on paper.
